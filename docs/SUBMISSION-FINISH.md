## Inspiration / problem and solution

“We corrected both weeks.” A deposit arrives, but which week does it cover, and what remains unresolved? New records can change a wage account without changing its total.

WageProof supports an ordinary California hourly wage case. It retains sources, proposes a factual account and compares later evidence with the last reviewed version. The intended benefit is a case update whose changes, assumptions and remaining requests can be inspected and carried forward. It does not determine entitlement, send messages or amend a claim.

## What it does

The fictional demonstration follows a scoped difference of **$140 → $80 → $80**. One $60 gross correction has a $48 net receipt. Its later $25/$35 allocation changes the weekly differences to $55/$25 while the combined $80 stays unchanged. The allocation request is answered; payroll's supporting calculation and reconciliation are still needed.

Source inspection, focused payment decisions, attributed corrections and revision-specific review preserve that distinction. A portable ZIP carries the update, supplement, selected originals and extracted text, with relative source links. The prepared example exposes saved results; its downloads identify the fictional recorded run and technical review scope, not worker/practitioner approval. Starting afresh creates a separate case for real analysis. These prepared-only labels do not change accounting, invoke a model or alter ordinary-case exports.

## How it was built / tech stack and credits

Sources supply the recorded statements; Poppler/Tesseract provide local text extraction and labelled OCR. The model proposes a typed interpretation, retaining unknowns with tools disabled. Code validates schema/exact quote anchors, calculates in integer cents, tracks review and builds exports. Matching quotes establish traceability, not correctness or authenticity.

Lockfile versions: React/React DOM **19.3.0**, Vite **7.3.6**, Ajv **8.20.0**, Lucide React **0.468.0**, Marked **18.0.14**; transitive dependencies are also recorded. ## Challenges / evidence and limitations

Candidate3 primary short outputs had 22 passes, two minor issues and one material omission; accepted analysis/detailed outputs and the strong ordinary workflow passed all 25 targets. Candidate4's deterministic repair passed those 25 known targets without model calls. Two previously unused reserve families then passed all eight targets in both workflows; the app used five attempts for four stages, including one exact-quote retry. Original failures and observation sets remain separate. Targets are not independent cases or accuracy estimates.

This small synthetic evaluation establishes neither semantic superiority, equal compute nor human savings. No worker/practitioner study establishes demand or agency usefulness. Prepared inspection adds no model evaluation. Inference takes minutes, requires the configured service and is not offline; monetary cost is unknown. Some updates span several pages and need the supplement. Setup was exercised on macOS, not as a public multi-user service.
