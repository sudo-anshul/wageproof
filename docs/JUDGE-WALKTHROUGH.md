1. Choose **Inspect the prepared example** (`#/case/prepared-example`). **“A recorded run, open for inspection.”** identifies saved fictional results. Browsing makes no model request; editing, importing, analysis and recording a review are unavailable in this fixed case.
2. Follow the three account stages:

   | Evidence stage | What to inspect |
   |---|---|
   | Starting account | Weekly differences of $80 and $60: **$140 combined** under retained premises. |
   | One correction | **$60 gross / $48 net received**, counted once; **$80 combined** remains, with weekly allocation still uncertain. |
   | Allocation supplied | $25 to Week A and $35 to Week B; weekly differences become **$55 and $25**, still **$80 combined**. |

3. Open the earnings advice, bank receipt and allocation table beside their assessments. Separate recorded statements, model interpretation and code arithmetic. Quotes/hashes support traceability, not legal truth.

5. Open **Prepare update**, or the portable example. Unzip before opening `update.html`; follow a source link and supplement.
