# WageProof: final synthetic evaluation

**Candidate4 passed all eight fresh reserve targets in both workflows, and its deterministic re-exports repaired all 25 known primary targets. The original candidate3 short outputs still have one material failure and two minor issues in the record. These results do not establish app superiority or human savings.**

Each family had initial evidence followed by later evidence. It included no participant cases. ## What failed, and what the repair establishes

Candidate1 D2 later's short output omitted the newly supplied service dates even though its analysis and detailed supplement retained them. A separate deterministic export repair fixed that omission; the first failed artifact remains retained. D1 initial required a citation repair before its first visible proposal.

In the primary candidate3 run, T3 later's standalone short output materially omitted the substance of a new worker-reported work allegation, its explicit undated/untimed limits and the targeted next request. The older first-three issue/action selection cap caused that loss; footer or indirect employer-nonresponse wording did not supply the missing factual update. T2 and T4 later had minor partly answered request ambiguities caused by stale compound titles without current detail. These were export failures; accepted stored analysis and detailed artifacts passed all 25 required semantic targets. The evaluator's provisional T3 short pass was corrected after independent review.

Candidate4 changes only `domain/portable.mjs`, `server/export-bundle.mjs` and `tests/portable.test.mjs` relative to candidate3. It prioritizes new or changed unresolved issues/actions against the selected revision's named reviewed baseline, carries consequential current detail and shows the remaining portion/status of partly answered requests. Client assets, extraction, model, prompt, validation, accounting and stored analysis are unchanged. Root reported 80 tests passing and a successful build before freezing it.

The candidate4 regression re-exported the eight unchanged saved candidate3 cases and original source bytes. Only `update.md`, `update.html` and manifests changed; analysis, calculation, review/comparison data, detailed supplements, evidence and source companions stayed unchanged. Independent review passed all 25 targets, checked all 62 new/changed active issue/action details, verified eight archives and 1,744 relative HTML links, and rechecked 128 original artifact bindings. The larger short outputs can span several pages. This is a known-state renderer regression, not fresh model performance. An initial re-export helper import-path error failed before loading cases or making outputs/model calls; the original helper and correction are retained.

The fresh candidate4 check used two previously unused reserve families after the new freeze and independent regression gate. R1 tested adopted daily/weekly arithmetic, a signed period excess and a partly answered compound request. R2 tested an unreceived scheduled correction, an unrelated equal-value receipt and a later received replacement. Both workflows passed all eight required meanings; no material or minor target error was observed. Independent mechanical review verified four ZIPs, 751 relative links, 188 accepted quote anchors and 64 unchanged first-artifact bindings. Presentation limits remain: some short-output calculation/exclusion explanations and retired-action history require the supplement. These bounded fresh results do not erase candidate3 failures or make the repaired primary outputs fresh observations.

## Infrastructure failures and staging qualification

Candidate1 ordinary D1 startup failed before inference in 0.386 seconds with `Operation not permitted`, no proposal and no usage. Candidate2 ordinary D1/D2 initial attempts invoked the model but their local source reads failed with `sandbox-exec: sandbox_apply: Operation not permitted`. Their explicit blocked notes, 27.005/33.372 seconds, 51,360/51,399 input tokens and 659/684 output tokens are retained; later evidence was not released to those attempts. Exit zero and a saved blocked note were not scored as a completed task.

Protocol v3 replaced the nested sandbox with one native Codex permission profile. One real benign model/tool smoke then passed in 17.435 seconds, separately from cases (52,036 input, 34,331 cached input, 17,501 cache-write input, 283 output and 116 reasoning tokens). Non-model preflight checked staged reads, immutable sources and earlier outputs, protected app/private/other-workspace/history boundaries, scratch writes, denied tool network, PNG OCR and image-only PDF rendering/OCR. Ordinary diagnostic D1 initial/later and primary T1 later each recovered from a local Python/xcrun command failure within the same retained run. No successful case observation replaces those failures.

The installed native sandbox permitted access under `/private/tmp` despite requested denies. This finding is path-specific. A later audit found that candidate3 PDF extraction created transient rasters under its actual macOS `TMPDIR`, contradicting the protocol's blanket claim that every derived artifact used non-temp storage. An exact harmless-marker read probe under that actual directory was denied, as was a probe under protected app scratch. No cross-workflow read or contamination was demonstrated; the `/private/tmp` exception does not imply access to every system-temp path. An initial contrary probe narrative was corrected, with the original preserved.

Candidate4 explicitly used `evaluation/upgrade/runtime/app-tmp/upgrade-candidate-4`. Its selected `TMPDIR` field, frozen PID and candidate freeze were checked and bound in `public/diagnostics/upgrade-candidate-4/RUNTIME-TMPDIR-RECEIPT.json`; no general environment or credentials were printed. The protected scratch read-denial probe passed. This qualifies the old protocol/configuration wording without rewriting frozen files or claiming universal sandbox secrecy.

## Review roles, provenance and claim limits

The evaluator authored the corpus after reading implementation and earlier failure classes, ran the workflows and made provisional assessments. A separate non-builder reviewer, `/root/upgrade_key_review`, challenged the final source bytes and answer keys before the corpus was sealed. Historical draft labels inside the keys remain; the independently reviewed seal is authoritative. Builders did not receive unused reserve facts or keys. Each workflow received later evidence only after its own initial artifact was saved, hashed and checked. Its later context contained its own earlier work, never the other workflow's output or a key-derived correction.

Output reviewer `/root/final_media_audit` was separate from corpus authorship and key review, but had contributed earlier UI work and saw evaluator summaries/flagged concerns. This was neither fully blind nor practitioner-independent review. Complete reserve pairs were released to that reviewer only after both workflows finished both stages. The review inspected raw/stored/portable meaning, relevant image-bearing sources and mechanical bindings; it did not certify every rendered screen or print state. Root's separate layout and setup checks are reported elsewhere.

Initial app outputs were unreviewed. The evaluator then added an explicitly technical lifecycle marker solely to exercise later comparison. That marker was not worker/practitioner approval or a key-derived correction. Later app outputs created new unreviewed revisions while preserving earlier source bytes, accepted analyses and technical review history. Ordinary earlier output bytes also remained unchanged. Exporting a packet did not send, file, amend or settle a claim.

The source/corpus manifest, freezes, seal, summary receipts and exact output-review hashes are bound by `FINAL-EVALUATION.json`. Retained raw evidence is excluded from the handoff: `evaluation/upgrade/runs/`, `evaluation/upgrade/baseline/records/`, `evaluation/upgrade/private/assessments/` and `work/upgrade/technical-independent-review/`. Those are local provenance paths, not downloadable evidence links. No raw answer keys, provider logs or private worker records are included.

The corpus is small, synthetic and purposefully constructed. Target passes establish bounded agreement with the reviewed evidence and representation policy; they do not certify record authenticity, legal applicability, representative accuracy, worker outcomes, adoption, field savings or whole-product reliability. No participant study, legal/practitioner validation, public upload or outreach occurred in this evaluation. The final candidate's fresh reserve observations and repaired known-state exports must remain distinct from the original primary failures.
